## Origin

DDKaraoke was built from PiKaraoke (https://github.com/vicwomg/pikaraoke), a karaoke server for Raspberry Pi, and its page layout and behaviour follow that project. The page style files, fireworks code, music and score sounds that were carried over have since been replaced with new ones. At the time of writing, the PiKaraoke repository does not show a licence file. If you are the author and want something changed or removed, please open an issue.
