Get DDKaraoke on your TV

A karaoke app for Android TV. Everyone picks songs from their phone.

Easiest way: use the free "Downloader" app

  1. On your TV, open the app store and install Downloader (free, by AFTVnews). It works on Google TV, Android TV and Fire TV.
  2. Open Downloader. In the box for the web address, type this page's address, then press Go.
  3. On this page, choose Download DDKaraoke and wait for it to finish.
  4. If the TV says installing is blocked, press Settings, switch on Allow for Downloader, go back, and press Install.
  5. Open DDKaraoke. Read the note, then press I understand with your remote.

You can delete the downloaded file afterwards. The app stays installed.

Good to know

  • Where it works: Android TV and Google TV (tested on an NVIDIA Shield). Fire TV and Android tablets or phones used as the screen should work too, but they have not been tested yet.
  • How to use it: the TV shows a QR code. Guests scan it with their phone to pick songs. No app is needed on their phones.
  • Songs: the app does not come with any karaoke songs or videos. The person hosting chooses where songs come from and is responsible for what they use.
  • Updates: to update, download the newest file from this page and install it the same way. Your settings stay.
